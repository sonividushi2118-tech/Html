My Favourite Dish Recipe

My favourite dish is pasta,i like them but the one i like the most is - White sauce pasta

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Veggies
  • White sauce base
  • seasoning
  • Cheese

Instructions

  1. Boil the Pasta
  2. Sauté the Vegetables
  3. melt 2 tablespoons of butter over low heat. Add the all-purpose flour and whisk continuously for 1-2 minutes until the raw smell is gone
  4. Stir in the salt, black pepper, chili flakes, Italian seasoning, and your cheese
  5. Once the cheese melts and the sauce is smooth, add the cooked pasta and sautéed vegetables
  6. If the sauce becomes too thick, splash in a little milk
